MapleCare Hospital Analysis using Power BI

MapleCare Hospital Performance Analysis Dashboard. This project is a healthcare analytics solution developed in Power BI to evaluate hospital performance across Clinical, Operational, and Financial domains. The project involved designing a Star Schema data model, transforming data with Power Query, and developing DAX measures to generate key performance indicators, including patient outcomes, revenue, profitability, mortality, readmission rates, average length of stay, and operational efficiency. The dashboards provide actionable insights into clinical performance, hospital operations, and financial health, enabling data-driven decision-making through intuitive visualizations and business intelligence reporting. Tools & Technologies: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Star Schema Data Modelling, Data Visualization, Business Intelligence.

Dermalife Oncology & Research Institute (DORI) Project using SQL

This project was conducted to analyze the impact of lifestyle factors on skin cancer outcomes. I used SQL to investigate the relationship between smoking, alcohol consumption, and skin-cancer outcomes. Analysis showed that 27.42% of smokers had severe diagnoses compared with 15.22% of drinkers, while 82.14% of patients who both smoked and drank had malignant diagnoses. The project applied SQL joins, conditional logic, aggregation, window functions, and percentage analysis to identify behavioral risk patterns and support evidence-based healthcare insights. Insights and recommendations were provided after the analysis to inform decision making and improve patient outcomes. Tools & Technologies: SQL, Data Analysis, Healthcare Analytics, Behavioral Risk Assessment, Evidence-Based Decision Making.
